# LeadReacher product context

## Register

product

## Users

B2B teams configuring outreach from their organization's website and business context. Onboarding supports deliberate decisions about audience, content, messaging, subscription, and channel connections.

## Product Purpose

Turn business context into a persisted campaign configuration and an operational workspace. Configuration is separate from launch. Payment or account connection must never imply that outreach has been sent.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, premium. The approved onboarding direction is a purposeful journey in which each decision becomes part of the next task, not a decorative presentation that delays work.

## Anti-references

Avoid gratuitous page slides, floating assets without destinations, blank transition frames, dramatic zooms, duplicate shells, fabricated media, and animation that competes with reading or keyboard access.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the existing visual system and shared CampaignCanvas anchors.
- Carry meaningful identity between tasks only when both sides exist.
- Keep response immediate, with one coordinated transition per interaction.
- Reflect successful persistence and confirmed billing, never predict success.
- Preserve the approved How LeadReacher Works sequence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, focus restoration, screen-reader semantics, and reduced-motion behavior. Desktop content must remain clear of fixed navigation; mobile keeps natural document flow. Motion must not hide required content or duplicate interactive controls.
